How it started

Built in Kampala.
Built for Kampala.

DigitalCart started from a simple frustration, great products existed across Uganda, but buying them online meant fighting through dollar prices, sketchy payment pages, and zero local support when something went wrong.

We set out to build the platform we always wished existed: everything priced in UGX, payments Ugandans actually use, and a customer service team that answers in the same timezone.

Today, DigitalCart connects thousands of buyers with verified sellers across electronics, fashion, furniture, and more, all from one straightforward platform.
